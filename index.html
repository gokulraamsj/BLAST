ABOUT THE SYMPOSIUM

Where curiosity hits escape velocity

BLAST is a national-level technical symposium bringing together students, researchers, and builders from across the country for three days of hackathons, technical paper presentations, robotics battles, design sprints, and workshops led by industry practitioners.

Our objective is simple: compress a year of learning, networking, and building into one high-energy weekend — and send every participant back with a working prototype, a new collaborator, or a sharper idea than the one they walked in with.

  • ✓ Industry mentors & expert judging panels
